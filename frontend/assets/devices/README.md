# Device marks

AuraLAN renders device-family marks locally and never fetches logo assets at runtime.

## Apple TV

The Apple TV mark geometry used by `frontend/js/icons.js` is sourced from the Simple Icons project.

- Upstream: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- Simple Icons license: CC0-1.0
- Apple TV and the Apple logo are trademarks of Apple Inc.
- Purpose: identify locally detected Apple TV devices; inclusion does not imply endorsement of AuraLAN.

The robot-vacuum, garage-door, heat-pump, VR-headset, and other generic device-family pictograms are AuraLAN-local presentation glyphs rather than third-party brand marks.
