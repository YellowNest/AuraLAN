# Service marks

AuraLAN bundles service marks locally. The interface never loads third-party logo assets at runtime.

## Pi-hole

`pihole.svg` is based on the Pi-hole project mark from the upstream `pi-hole/web` repository.

- Copyright: Pi-hole, LLC
- Upstream: https://github.com/pi-hole/web/blob/master/img/logo.svg
- Source project license: EUPL-1.2
- Purpose: identify a locally detected Pi-hole service

## Docker, WireGuard, and Caddy

The monochrome glyph geometry in `docker.svg`, `wireguard.svg`, and `caddy.svg` is sourced from the Simple Icons project.

- Upstream: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- Simple Icons license: CC0-1.0
- The product names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
- Purpose: identify locally detected services; inclusion does not imply endorsement of AuraLAN.

These marks are intentionally kept separate from AuraLAN's own brand assets.
